Colours:

Info:
In Dutch, colours behave like adjectives and follow the rule where
"de"-words + plurals get an -e (I call this Var2) and "het" words get nothing
(I call this Var1).

Materials:
SnR contains "Colours" that are actually materials, these behave differently 
as they are technically not colours (duh). Materials (typically) end with the noun + "-en",
note words like "Steen" (Stone) turn into "Sten(en)" due to how vowels behave as well. 
(I'll list these words under Var2)
One exception: in the "<COLOUR> on Black/White" collections, they should be the normal noun.
(I'll list these under Var1)

I took Brown as an example here. Also added some comments underneath
some translations where clarification might be nice.

After each translation I put (Var1) or (Var2) to signal which 
variant of the colour translation to use accordingly. If it's a 
material (as explained before) you always use Var2, unless explicitly
stated otherwise indicated by "MVar1"
